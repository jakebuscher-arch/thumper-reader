A Rainy Day
RAIN and damp and puddles many
Never stop the games of bunnies
On what may seem a dreary day
the Bunnies play the day away
But one such bunny wasn't there
He wasn't invited, wasn't fair
See, not all bunnies are so kind
as friends of you or friends of mine.
Thumper was a different type
he liked not to play, instead to write
The other bunnies where fast and sleek
Thumper liked to eat and eat.
Out of his window Thumper gazed
His Imagination was set ablaze
All he had to do today
imagine a new friend with whom to play
Not a normal bunny will do
What If bunnies with wings flew!
Soaring though the dreary skies
Flapping wings and soaring for miles

Option 1: Kelmscott Foliate Woodcut

Arts & Crafts Movement / William Morris Private Press Heritage

Engraved in deep warm sepia ink with delicate white woodcut veins and antique gold berry clusters. Dense, stylized acanthus foliage curls at the four corners, anchored by classical concentric rules and rhythmic leaf modules along all outer edges.

Aesthetic Mood: Master craftsman storybook, timeless literary gravitas.
Corner Treatment: Heavy dual-tone acanthus leaf with gold core rosette.
Edge Framing: Continuous undulating foliate ribbon with crimson-and-gold berries.
Best Suited For: Readers who love antique, high-end collector folio editions.
